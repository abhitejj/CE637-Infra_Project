The value function has marginal savings of INR 1,022.90/MT for 21,000 < C2 < 32,000 MT, INR 706.55/MT for 32,000 < C2 < 34,000 MT, and zero above 34,000 MT. At 32,000 MT Gandhidham-to-Surat reaches zero. The second regime is the exchange Chhani-to-Surat + Sabarmati-to-Banaskantha replacing Sabarmati-to-Surat + Gandhidham-to-Banaskantha: 743.55 - 487.20 + 900.70 - 450.50 = 706.55 INR/MT. At 34,000 MT capacity is still binding; left/right marginal savings are 706.55/0, and stock prevents further gain. Only above this point is dispatch capacity slack. The solver-selected dual at either kink is one valid subgradient, not a unique two-sided derivative.

Baseline-to-34,000 savings: INR 5,504,700.00/month; INR 66,056,400.00/year, assuming the same scenario repeats. No construction cost or payback has been evaluated.
